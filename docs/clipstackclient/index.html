CopyPouch Pocket

CopyPouch Pocket brings your CopyPouch text history to iPhone and iPad.
Sync from your Mac, search the history on the go, and copy what you need with one tap.

Overview

A lightweight companion for checking CopyPouch history away from your Mac.

Keep your frequently reused text history close at hand on iPhone or iPad.
Local clips are also available when you want to save something directly on the device.

  • Automatic sync from CopyPouch on macOS
  • Search synced history on iPhone or iPad
  • Copy clips with one tap
  • Browse All, Local, and synced history sources
  • Search history on the device
  • Save local text clips when needed
Note: To use sync with CopyPouch on macOS,
sign in on iPhone or iPad with the same iCloud account used on your Mac.
